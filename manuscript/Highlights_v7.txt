Highlights

- Mean per-class one-vs-rest accuracy is 1-2e/K and stays above 83.3% for 12 classes
- Holding out groups of spectra drops SVM accuracy from 97% to 88% (12 varieties)
- With groups held out, SVM, ANN and LDA no longer differ
- Only SFS beats random band subsets of equal size, and only with 5-10 bands
- Band-selection results should be benchmarked against random subsets
